# Contributing

All help is welcome: bug reports, ideas, new excuses the judge should know, typo fixes, art, or code.

## Quick ways to help
- **The judge got it wrong?** Open an issue with the charge, your excuse, and the ruling you expected. Those make the judge smarter.
- **Found a bug or something hard to read or use?** Open an issue and say what you did and what happened. A screenshot helps.
- **Want to change code?** Fork the repo, make your change on a branch, and open a pull request.

## Running it locally
It's one file: open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

## Accessibility
Please keep it playable for everyone: everything should work with a keyboard and on a phone, text should stay readable, and motion should calm down when "reduce motion" is turned on.

Be kind. The judge roasts excuses, never people.
